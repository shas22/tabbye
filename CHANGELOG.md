## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Automatic archiving of HTTP(S) tabs after 1–365 days without a visit (seven by default), with a five-minute grace period after startup or a long gap.
- Protection for active, highlighted, pinned, loading, and audible tabs, plus excluded websites and their subdomains.
- Toolbar popup and full archive page with search, date groups, pagination, reopen, and confirmed delete.
- Options page for the timeout, exclusions, and JSON backup export/import.
- Lifetime count of automatically archived tabs.
- Pause/resume and **Skip wait** controls.
- <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> shortcut to open the popup.
